MONITORING CAPABILITY

Environmental Awareness

RAKSALA uses environmental sensing to maintain awareness of conditions that can affect electronics and communication equipment in high-altitude environments.

Temperature Monitoring

Tracks ambient temperature to help identify extreme cold and thermal operating conditions.

Atmospheric Monitoring

Humidity and pressure measurements provide environmental context for high-altitude operation.

Exposure Monitoring

UV index and dust measurements help identify environmental exposure conditions around the system.

WHO WE ARE

About RAKSALA

RAKSALA is a smart, rugged and adaptive protection system designed for equipment operating in high-altitude and high-stress environments.

The system combines environmental monitoring, adaptive physical protection, intelligent power management and controlled communication into a unified platform.

RAKSALA is designed around the principle of maintaining system awareness and operational continuity even when the surrounding environment becomes extremely challenging.

RAKSALA TECHNOLOGY

System Architecture

An integrated architecture designed to protect, power, monitor and connect critical systems in high-altitude environments.

THE RAKSALA SYSTEM

Four Integrated Layers

RAKSALA combines physical protection, environmental awareness, resilient power and controlled communication into a unified field system.

01
PROTECTION LAYER

Adaptive Protection Skin

The outer protective structure forms the first line of defence for the RAKSALA system in harsh high-altitude environments.

A layered TPU-based protective approach is designed to reduce exposure to moisture, dust, UV radiation, abrasion and extreme environmental conditions.

  • Multi-layer protective construction
  • UV and weather resistance
  • Moisture and dust protection
  • Reduced environmental stress on internal electronics
02
MONITORING LAYER

Environmental Sensor Layer

The sensor layer provides continuous awareness of the surrounding environment and operating conditions.

RAKSALA focuses on five key environmental parameters that can influence system operation in high-altitude locations.

🌡 Temperature 💧 Humidity ◉ Pressure ☀ UV Index 🌫 Dust

Sensor data can be processed locally before being forwarded through the authorized communication architecture.

03
ENERGY LAYER

Intelligent Power System

RAKSALA uses an integrated power architecture designed to support operation in remote environments where conventional power infrastructure may not be available.

Solar energy can be managed through an MPPT charge controller and stored in a battery system with battery-management protection.

☀ Solar → MPPT → Battery + BMS → System
  • Solar energy harvesting
  • MPPT-based charging
  • Battery management
  • Power distribution to system modules
04
COMMUNICATION LAYER

RAKSALA Private Communication Hub

The RAKSALA Hub is designed as the local communication and processing point between deployed RAKSALA nodes and the authorized control infrastructure.

The proposed architecture emphasizes authenticated devices, encrypted communication, local processing and controlled data flow.
